# AI use

AI assisted with the following parts of the project:

- **Application development:** Writing and refining frontend, backend, and data-processing code.
- **Debugging and testing:** Troubleshooting issues, reviewing code, and developing automated tests.
- **Data integration:** Researching elevation and ephemeris data formats and implementing their use in the application.
- **Calculation logic:** Developing and checking calculations for Sun and Earth positions, terrain obstruction, visibility over time, and solar-power estimates.
- **Documentation and presentation:** Drafting and refining technical explanations, submission text, and interface and presentation layouts.

The project uses the data sources credited in the [README](README.md#data-assumptions-and-credits). Original project images were created without AI image generation.
